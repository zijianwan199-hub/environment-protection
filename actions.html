Everyday Actions

Environmental protection becomes easier when people focus on specific choices. These actions are simple, realistic, and useful for students, families, and small communities.

Our Actions for a Better Planet

Reduce Waste

Recycle materials, compost when possible, and avoid single-use products.

Save Energy

Turn off lights, unplug devices, and choose efficient appliances.

Use Less

Choose reusable items and buy only what you need.
